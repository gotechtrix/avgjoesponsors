About

The Average Joe's MBA runs live, in-person masterclasses that give small business owners and leaders practical, real-world tools without the jargon, high costs, or wasted time. Sessions are built around what is actually working right now, the mistakes that cost real money, and simple systems for stronger lead flow and smarter growth decisions.

Two signature formats headline the calendar: The Secret Sauce, a rapid-fire business masterclass, and Introduction to AI, a beginner-friendly session on using AI in a real business. Events are hosted around the greater Sacramento area and are made possible by local business sponsors.

The Average Joe's MBA is a live, in-person business education series for small business owners and leaders, delivering practical, real-world tools without the jargon. Learn more at averagejoemba.com.
