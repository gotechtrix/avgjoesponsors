Hosted at Outlet Coworking and sponsored by JR Galicha (Ignite LG). Schedule: Doors open at 10:00 AM for coffee, light refreshments, and time to meet other local business owners. The presentation starts at 10:30 AM and runs about 45 minutes, followed by Q&A and open networking. Venue: 1180 Iron Point Rd, Folsom, CA 95630.
