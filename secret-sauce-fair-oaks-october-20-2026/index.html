About the event

Everyone sees the highlight reel. Very few people see the recipe. The Secret Sauce is a live, in-person workshop with simple, low-cost marketing steps we've used to generate leads for our own businesses.

Presenters: Chris Camara (founder of Cobalt Insurance Agency) and Brandon Walcott (founder of Techtrix).

You'll walk out with:

  • A checklist of brand basics every business needs before spending money on marketing
  • Simple habits that help local customers find you online
  • How to get your emails delivered and noticed

Schedule: Doors open at 10:00 AM for coffee, light refreshments, and networking. The presentation starts at 10:30 AM and runs about 45 minutes, followed by Q&A and open networking.

Ready to learn the recipe?
